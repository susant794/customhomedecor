# SEO Strategy for customhomedecor.in

## ✅ Completed (Foundation)
1. **robots.txt** - Allows search engines to crawl your site properly
2. **sitemap.xml** - Helps Google discover all your pages
3. **Meta Tags** - Added description, keywords, author, theme-color
4. **Open Graph Tags** - Improves social media sharing appearance
5. **Twitter Card Tags** - Optimizes Twitter/X sharing
6. **Canonical Tags** - Prevents duplicate content issues
7. **JSON-LD Structured Data** - Rich snippets for better SERP appearance
8. **Improved Title Tag** - Better keyword targeting

---

## 🔥 Priority Actions (Implement Now)

### 1. **Submit to Google Search Console**
- Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
- Add your domain: `customhomedecor.in`
- Verify ownership (add DNS record)
- Submit your sitemap: `https://customhomedecor.in/sitemap.xml`
- Monitor indexation and fix crawl errors

### 2. **Verify Business Information**
- Register on **Google My Business** (now Google Business Profile)
- Update phone number and address in index.html JSON-LD
- Add hours of operation if applicable

### 3. **Optimize Each Page**
Create unique meta descriptions and titles for each route:

#### HomePage
- **Title**: "Custom Home Decor - Interior & Exterior Design Studio | Modern Affordable Design"
- **Meta Description**: "Explore our portfolio of stunning interior and exterior designs. Premium design services with affordable pricing. Get your free design consultation today."

#### InteriorPage
- **Title**: "Interior Design Services | Custom Home Decor Studio"
- **Meta Description**: "Professional interior design for residential & commercial spaces. Browse our interior portfolio and transform your space with our expert designers."

#### ExteriorPage
- **Title**: "Exterior Design Services | Modern Landscape & Facade Design"
- **Meta Description**: "Expert exterior design services including landscaping, facade, and outdoor spaces. View our portfolio of stunning exterior transformations."

#### AboutPage
- **Title**: "About Custom Home Decor | Award-Winning Design Studio"
